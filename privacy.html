Privacy Policy for Box Blast 2D

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Box Blast 2D: Block Puzzle ("the Game"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

Box Blast 2D does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No account registration is required to play.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location data, or device identifiers.
  • Game progress (high scores and best records) is saved locally on your device storage only.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Game may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdMob) to offer optional in-game rewards (e.g. board revival). These advertising partners may use non-personalized, anonymous device identifiers to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and COPPA/GDPR regulations.

3. Children's Privacy

Box Blast 2D is designed for all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Offline Gameplay

The core gameplay is 100% functional offline without requiring an active internet connection.
