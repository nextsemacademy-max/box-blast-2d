### 🎮 How to Play
1. **Drag & Place**: Pick shapes from the bottom dock and drag them onto the 8×8 socket board.
2. **Blast Lines**: Fill any horizontal row or vertical column completely to blast the marbles.
3. **Chain Combos**: Clear lines turn after turn to build massive combo multipliers (`NICE!`, `COOL!`, `AMAZING!`, `MASTERPIECE!`).
4. **Plan Ahead**: Always leave space for 3×3 clusters — the game ends when no moves fit!
