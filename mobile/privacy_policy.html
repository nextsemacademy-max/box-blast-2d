Privacy Policy for Box Blast 2D

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Box Blast 2D ("the Game") provided by NextSem Academy ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect

Box Blast 2D is designed as a standalone offline casual puzzle game. We prioritize your privacy:

  • No Personal Information: We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal identifying information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts).
  • Local Device Storage: Game progress, high scores, adventure level stars, and audio/haptic preferences are saved strictly locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services

The core game operates 100% offline and does not integrate any third-party tracking SDKs or behavioral ad networks. If in the future Google Play Services or AdMob are integrated, they will process data strictly in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies.

3. Children’s Privacy

Box Blast 2D is family-friendly and safe for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The game contains no chat, user-to-user interaction, or external data sharing.

4. Permissions

The game requests the following standard Android permissions:

  • VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during piece placements and line clears (can be toggled off in settings).
  • INTERNET: Used by the internal WebView localhost server to load local offline game assets.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
